# Intellectual and software provenance

The SS-iTRCA framework follows Wang et al., DOI: 10.1109/JBHI.2025.3577813. TRCA follows Nakanishi et al., DOI: 10.1109/TBME.2017.2694818. This repository contains the local implementation used in the author's experiments, with AF and MP-GLA modifications; it does not claim that the inherited framework is new.

NumPy, SciPy and threadpoolctl are external dependencies governed by their own licences. They are not vendored here.

The copied SSVEP Analysis Toolbox files and the TransRCA port from the wider private research workspace are NOT included in this release candidate because their redistribution terms have not been completed in this review. Refer to https://github.com/pikipity/SSVEP-Analysis-Toolbox and https://github.com/WenliLan/SSVEP-TransRCA for those separate baseline implementations. This release therefore does not claim to reproduce all independent baseline comparisons from a fresh environment.

No raw EEG, participant demographic files, unpublished manuscript draft, account credentials or private absolute data paths are included. Dataset availability and licences remain with their original providers. The MIT licence applies to wu's original software and does not relicense third-party data or code.
